Tasks

4.
Create a servlet named ProductController that acts as the Controller in MVC, handling requests to add a new product and forwarding to product-list.jsp for display.
5.
Briefly explain in your own words how the MVC + DAO pattern you just implemented is similar to the architecture used in Spring MVC framework, focusing on the separation of Controller, View, Model, and DAO layers.
